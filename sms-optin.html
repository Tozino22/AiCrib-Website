SMS COMMUNICATIONS

SMS Opt-In.

Last updated: June 13, 2026

AICrib, a subsidiary of TeeHay Consulting Inc, uses SMS messaging to provide convenient communication with our clients. By opting in to our SMS communications, you consent to receive text messages from us at the phone number you provide.

What Messages Will You Receive?

By opting in, you may receive the following types of SMS messages from AICrib / TeeHay Consulting:

  • Payment links — Secure Stripe payment links for consulting services and invoices.
  • Appointment confirmations — Reminders and confirmations for scheduled consultations.
  • Service updates — Important updates about your active projects or engagements.
  • Account notifications — Order confirmations, delivery status, and account-related alerts.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your engagement with our services. Typically, you may receive 1–5 messages per month. You will never receive unsolicited marketing messages via SMS.

Message & Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply. Your carrier's standard messaging rates will apply to any messages you send or receive. Please contact your wireless carrier for details about your messaging plan.

How to Opt Out

You can opt out at any time. Simply reply STOP to any SMS message you receive from us, and you will be unsubscribed immediately. You will receive a one-time confirmation message confirming your opt-out.

After opting out, you will no longer receive SMS messages from AICrib / TeeHay Consulting unless you re-subscribe.
